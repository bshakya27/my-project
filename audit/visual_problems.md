# Top 5 Visual Problems

## 1. **No Impact Feedback on Actions** (Critical)

### The Issue
- Shooting, hitting enemies, repairing, upgrading, and building all feel silent and invisible
- No particle effects, screen shake, or animation confirm that your action worked
- Players get no satisfying feedback loop

### Why This Matters
Immediate visual feedback is fundamental to game feel. Without it, the game feels unresponsive and disconnected, even when the mechanics are working perfectly.

### What Players Experience
- Click to shoot → projectile travels silently with no presence
- Enemy takes a hit → just loses HP with no visual reaction
- Enemy dies → vanishes instantly, feels like a glitch
- Click to repair core → health bar updates but core doesn't react
- Right-click to build → tower appears without fanfare
- Click to upgrade → number changes but nothing visually happens

### Impact on Polish
**Visual maturity loss: -2 points**

---

## 2. **Projectiles Lack Presence** (High Impact)

### The Issue
- Projectiles are tiny (4px radius) plain circles
- Player projectiles (yellow) and tower projectiles (light blue) blend into the background
- No trails, glow, or speed lines make them feel fast or dangerous
- Hard to see where shots are coming from or going to

### Why This Matters
Projectiles are the main visual output of player skill and tower effectiveness. If they're invisible, the core combat feedback is dead.

### Specific Problems
- **Too small** – At a glance, you can't see them unless they're passing right in front of you
- **No trails** – No visual line from source to target
- **No glow** – They don't stand out from the green background
- **No speed indicator** – Doesn't feel like they're moving at 600 px/sec

### Impact on Polish
**Visual maturity loss: -1.5 points**

---

## 3. **Flat, Empty Background** (High Impact)

### The Issue
- Game world is just a solid green-brown rectangle
- No environment detail, texture, or depth
- No parallax or layering to create visual dimension
- Looks like a test scene, not a real village

### Why This Matters
The background sets the atmosphere and world. A flat background makes the entire game feel unfinished and two-dimensional (not in a good retro way).

### Missing Elements
- **Texture/detail** – Could have subtle patterns, subtle grid, or terrain variation
- **Parallax layers** – A distant layer (trees, hills) that moves slower creates depth
- **Atmospheric elements** – Subtle clouds, stars, or light effects
- **Village-like environment** – Small buildings, walls, or structures in the background
- **Lighting** – Shadows or vignetting to add mood and focus

### Impact on Polish
**Visual maturity loss: -1.5 points**

---

## 4. **Towers Are Visually Indistinct** (Medium Impact)

### The Issue
- All towers are circles with a slight color shift per level (blue → cyan → teal)
- No visual hierarchy or character differentiation between levels
- Doesn't feel like you're building an army; feels like you're placing colored blobs
- Level upgrades are invisible (only color changes slightly)

### Why This Matters
Towers are the player's main strategic choice. If they look the same at every level, upgrades feel invisible and unsatisfying.

### Specific Problems
- **Level 1, 2, 3 towers are too similar** – Same circle shape, only color/size vary
- **No visual "growth"** – A tower should look noticeably more powerful when upgraded
- **Missing detail** – Could have different designs per level (spikes, plating, charging effects)
- **Upgrade animation is missing** – Tower should flash, grow, or glow when upgraded

### Impact on Polish
**Visual maturity loss: -1 point**

---

## 5. **No Damage or Danger Visual States** (Medium Impact)

### The Issue
- Core and enemies have HP bars but no visual pain/stress states
- Core doesn't react visually when taking damage (no shake, flash, or warning)
- Enemies don't flinch or flash when hit
- No visual sense of danger as your core HP drops

### Why This Matters
Visual state feedback (pain, stress, danger) is critical for tension and immersion. Players need to *feel* threatened.

### Specific Problems
- **Core damage is silent** – Bar decreases but core doesn't visually suffer
- **No screen shake** – Hits on core should jolt the player
- **No red alert** – Core should glow red/orange as health drops (danger state)
- **Enemies never react** – No flinch, knockback, or flash on hit
- **No warning effects** – Player can't visually see that the core is about to die

### Impact on Polish
**Visual maturity loss: -0.5 points**
