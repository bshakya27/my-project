# Visual Upgrade Audit: The Last Village

## Executive Summary

**Overall Impression: Functional Prototype → Needs Premium Polish**

The game is currently **mechanically complete but visually minimal**. It reads as a working prototype with bare-bones placeholder aesthetics. With targeted visual improvements, it can feel significantly more polished and high-end without requiring a complete redesign.

### Current State
- ✅ Clear, readable UI with good contrast
- ✅ Distinct game objects with recognizable silhouettes
- ✅ Appropriate dark theme for a defense game
- ❌ Minimal visual feedback on player actions
- ❌ No particle effects or impact animations
- ❌ Flat, static environment
- ❌ Weak sense of visual hierarchy among upgradeable objects
- ❌ No animation polish on interactions

### Visual Maturity Level
**Current:** 3/10 (Early Prototype)  
**With Quick Wins:** 5/10 (Minimal Viable Product)  
**With High-Impact Upgrades:** 7/10 (Polished Indie Game)  
**Fully Polish:** 8-9/10 (Premium Quality)

---

## Key Findings

### What Works Well
1. **Color scheme** – The muted green/brown/blue palette is cohesive and appropriate for a village-defense theme
2. **Object distinctiveness** – The hexagon core, circular forge, and circular towers are visually different enough to identify at a glance
3. **UI clarity** – HUD text is readable with good contrast; no confusion about game state
4. **Player character** – Golden circle with aim-line is immediately understandable

### What Needs Improvement
1. **Visual feedback is nearly absent** – Shooting, hitting, dying, upgrading, and building all feel silent and unresponsive
2. **Projectiles are forgettable** – Tiny circles that zip across the screen without presence
3. **Enemy death is abrupt** – Enemies vanish instantly with only a gold drop; no sense of impact
4. **Towers lack personality** – They're all circles with different colors; no distinctive designs per level or type
5. **No environmental depth** – Background is flat; no parallax, atmosphere, or detail
6. **Upgrades feel invisible** – Core repair/tower upgrades happen instantly with no visual confirmation
7. **Screen lacks impact** – No screen shake, no visual feedback when the core takes damage
8. **UI is bland** – Dark boxes with text; functional but not premium-feeling
